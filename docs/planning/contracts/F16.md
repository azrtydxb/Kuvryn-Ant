# F16 — OCI artifacts contract

Status: Defined for implementation; not yet conformance-tested. Revision 1, 2026-10-18.

## Client and protocol baseline

OCI Distribution 1.1 non-container artifacts with subject/referrers discovery and media-type preservation.

- oras: **v1.3.4** ([release/source](https://api.github.com/repos/oras-project/oras/releases/latest)).

Pin actual downloaded artifact digests in the conformance fixture lock; these are deliberately selected baseline versions, not a promise of all historical or future clients.

## Publish / ingest

ORAS push/attach publishes manifests and referenced blobs; validate subject access and closure through the shared OCI core.

## Consume and source support

ORAS pull/discover/copy consumes artifacts and referrers from local/remote/replica repositories.

## Resolution policy

package-owner per OCI repository; digest-bound subject graph

## Format-specific integrity and metadata

Referrers are indexed without rewriting manifest bytes. GC and replication account for subject/referrer relationships. Distinguish artifact conformance from image conformance.

## Explicit non-goals

No server-side execution of artifacts or assertion that stored attestations are trusted without external verification.

## Required client scenarios

- [ ] Attach an artifact to an image digest, discover and pull it, then verify replica subject graph.
- [ ] Cross-scope subject access fails; deleting a subject cannot silently orphan protected retained attachments.

All [shared source, cache, auth, lifecycle, search, replication and conformance requirements](common.md) also apply. Task T1 closes contract definition only; T2–T4 remain implementation and evidence work.
